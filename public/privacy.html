Your workspace, on your device.

Omni Sticky is a free beta for personal planning. This page explains the behavior of this release.

Where your work is stored

Notes, tasks, projects, groups, canvas layouts, and preferences are stored in your browser profile using local storage. Uploaded canvas photos are compressed and stored in IndexedDB on the same device. The app does not send workspace contents to an application server or use them to train AI.

There is no account, cloud sync, collaboration, or automatic cloud backup. Different browsers, devices, web addresses, and the Windows app have separate workspaces.

Backups and deletion

Use Settings → Data & backups → Download complete backup. JSON backups include notes, projects, groups, canvas items, connections, and uploaded photos. Store backups securely: they are readable files, without encryption. Restore replaces the workspace after a confirmation.

Clearing site data, uninstalling a browser profile, or ending private browsing can delete local work. Browser storage protection can reduce automatic eviction but cannot prevent manual deletion. Delete individual notes in the app; clear this site's browser storage to remove all local data, including photos and preferences. Clear downloaded backups separately.

Network requests

Your hosting provider receives ordinary page and asset requests, which may include your IP address and browser information in its logs. The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics, remote fonts, or tracking cookies. Images from external web addresses in older or imported workspaces may contact their original hosts when displayed. Uploaded local photos stay on this device unless you export and share them.

Permissions and offline use

Notification permission and storage protection are optional. Scheduled task reminders and notifications while the app is closed are not available. The web app can reopen offline after its first successful online visit and offline cache setup; hosting must use HTTPS (or localhost). Offline data is still local and needs backups.

Beta limitations

Omni Sticky is designed for personal planning and works best on a larger screen. Focus timers operate while their view is open; leaving a timer view ends that session. Background browser scheduling can delay alerts. The app is not a guaranteed alarm service. Data is not encrypted by the app; people with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to read it.

Questions and feedback

See the feedback instructions. Send only the information you choose to share; never include private notes or backups in public feedback.
